Verified from schematic and PCB

Filter IS populated in the PCB layout: C9 100 nF (series), R6 15.8 kΩ (to ISO_GND), R7 2.32 kΩ (series), C10 1 nF (to ISO_GND). U5 OPA197 buffers FILTER_OUT to VCTRL. R6/C9 and R7/C10 form a loaded RC band-pass network.

Important: J2 is DAC_OUT + ISO_GND, not the buffered filtered output. The schematic labels U5 supply rails +15V_ISO and -15V_ISO, but an actual powered supply was not verified.

KiCad schematic review · integration status

Both supplied KiCad files identify C9 = 100 nF, R6 = 15.8 kΩ, R7 = 2.32 kΩ, C10 = 1 nF, and U5 = OPA197xD. The identified stage follows DAC_OUT → RC band-pass → FILTER_OUT → U5 buffer → VCTRL. This is a voltage-conditioning stage, not a regulated current source. J2 connects DAC_OUT and ISO_GND, not VCTRL. The schematic includes +15V_ISO and -15V_ISO rail labels; this does not establish a working physical supply. Real current output, frequency limits, and protection remain unverified. Bench-only, no human or animal connection.
